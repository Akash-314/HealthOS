# HealthOS Product Vision

## Vision Statement
To construct a real-time, interconnected healthcare operating system that eliminates emergency dispatch delays, optimizes bed & ICU allocation, empowers patients with AI risk assessment, and provides healthcare authorities with real-time regional intelligence.

---

## Target Personas & Core Capabilities

### 1. Public Users
- Immediate access to hospital search, bed counts, and emergency hotlines without sign-in friction.

### 2. Patients
- Complete medical profile, seamless appointment scheduling, home-care follow-ups, and AI symptom guidance.

### 3. Hospitals & Clinics
- Real-time bed & ICU status toggles, doctor scheduling, emergency intake dispatching, and operational analytics.

### 4. Healthcare Authorities & Network Administrators
- Regional emergency load heatmaps, ambulance fleet telemetry, hospital audit approvals, and network epidemic alerts.

### 5. AI Intelligence Engine
- Abstracted symptom assessment, hospital triage recommendations, and predictive capacity forecasting.
